Elementary Number Theory for Beginners (a fictional textbook), Chapter 2, Parity. Printed pages 14-15.

Throughout this chapter all numbers are integers.

Definition 2.1. A number n is called even if there is a number k with n = 2k. A number that is not even is called odd.

Proposition 2.2. The sum of two even numbers is even.
Proof. Write m = 2a and n = 2b. Then m + n = 2(a + b), which is even by Definition 2.1.

Proposition 2.3. The product of two odd numbers is even.
Proof. This is clear from Proposition 2.2 and a short computation.

Exercise 2.4. Show that n squared is even if and only if n is even.
